# Support

- Documentation: https://hyunter777.github.io/dungeon-shooter-maker-docs/
- Q&A: https://github.com/hyunter777/dungeon-shooter-maker-docs/discussions/categories/q-a
- Bug reports: https://github.com/hyunter777/dungeon-shooter-maker-docs/issues
- Publisher website: https://www.flycat.co.kr
- Email for private support details: hyunter@flycat.co.kr

Ask usage and integration questions in Q&A. Search existing posts first; if a reply solves your question, mark it as
the answer. Report reproducible defects through the bug report form in Issues.

Include your product version, full Unity Editor version, render pipeline, operating system, the content id or window
involved (if relevant), reproduction steps, expected/actual behavior and relevant Console messages. A screenshot is
useful. Public issues and discussions are visible to everyone: redact personal information, credentials and private
project paths. Do not upload the purchased package, product implementation, paid assets, fonts or a complete customer
project. Use private support to arrange any necessary confidential reproduction details.

These destinations are prepared for publication. This support policy does not promise a response time or announce
product availability.
