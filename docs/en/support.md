## Support

- Publisher website: <https://www.flycat.co.kr>
- Email: <hyunter@flycat.co.kr>

Ask usage and integration questions in Q&A, and report reproducible defects through the bug report form in Issues.

For a reproducible issue, include the product version, full Unity Editor version, render pipeline, operating system,
the content id or window involved, reproduction steps, expected and actual behaviour and the relevant Console
messages. A screenshot is useful.

Public posts are visible to everyone: remove personal information, credentials and private project paths, and do not
upload the purchased package, the product implementation, the sample content as a package, fonts or a complete
customer project. Use private support to arrange any necessary confidential reproduction details.

These destinations are prepared for publication. This support policy does not promise a response time or announce
product availability.
